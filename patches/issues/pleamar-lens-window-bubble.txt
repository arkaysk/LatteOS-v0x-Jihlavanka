TITLE
Feature request: blur behind a normal window where it asks (ext-background-effect), as for panels and menus

BODY
**Version:** pleamar-wm 0.6.9 on pleamar 0.7.10, in the pleamar-wm session on an AMD RX 640 (RADV POLARIS12).

LatteOS's apps (Quickshell) have a side panel of glass: what is behind the window should show through it, blurred, as
on Windows 11 (Mica/Acrylic). Quickshell 0.3.2 asks for it with `BackgroundEffect.blurRegion`
(`ext-background-effect-v1`). pleamar-wm takes that region from layer surfaces and popups (`nest.rs`, `layers::show`
with `blur`), and blurs with `blur passes …` from session.conf; a toplevel window's region is not used.

**What we tried instead, in the session scene:** a `body` with `glass` under the window. Without `lens` it does not
blur (`frost` is for a glass that bends). With `lens: true`, `refraction: 0%`:

- it frosts only what the scene itself painted (other windows), not the wallpaper below the scene, so under a side
  panel it shows as a darker rectangle where a window is behind and sharp wallpaper elsewhere;
- under a whole window a large rounded shape like a soap bubble, with a wavy edge that splits into a rainbow, shows
  through the window, even with `dispersion`, `highlight`, `mirror` and `ripple` at 0. With `frost: 0` it is gone.

**What we would like:** a toplevel window's blur region honoured as a panel's is (the same dual Kawase, the same
`blur` settings), ideally with its strength changeable while the session runs, so a settings page can offer a slider.

**What we do meanwhile:** the windows' glass is `lens: false` again (no blur, no bubble).
