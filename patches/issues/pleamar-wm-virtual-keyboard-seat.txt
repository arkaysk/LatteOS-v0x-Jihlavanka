TITLE
With `agent on`, wtype (zwp_virtual_keyboard_v1) types on an agent's seat: nothing has its keyboard, nothing arrives

BODY
**Version:** pleamar-wm 0.6.35 (`a9b803a`), on pleamar 0.7.31. Reproduced in `pleamar-wm headless`.

**What happens:** with `agent on` in session.conf, the session announces three `wl_seat`s: yours ("pleamar") first,
then the agents' ("cua-agent", "cua-agent-2"). wtype binds every seat it is told of and keeps the last one, so it asks
for its virtual keyboard on `cua-agent-2`. There nothing has the keyboard, and that seat's clipboard is the agent's own:
`wtype abc` types nothing into the window you are in, and `wtype -M ctrl -k v` pastes nothing. With `agent off` there
is one seat and wtype works. (`WAYLAND_DEBUG=1 wtype x` shows the three binds and `create_virtual_keyboard` on the last.)

LatteOS pastes what was chosen in its clipboard's list, and an emoji, with Ctrl+V through wtype; that never worked.

**Reproduce (headless):**

```sh
printf 'agent on\n' > /tmp/pwm/session.conf; : > /tmp/pwm/autostart
PLEAMAR_WM_CONFIG=/tmp/pwm/session.conf PLEAMAR_WM_AUTOSTART=/tmp/pwm/autostart pleamar-wm headless &
# its WAYLAND_DISPLAY is in its first lines
WAYLAND_DISPLAY=pleamar-2 foot sh -c 'cat > /tmp/pwm/got' &
sleep 2; printf pasted | WAYLAND_DISPLAY=pleamar-2 wl-copy
WAYLAND_DISPLAY=pleamar-2 wtype -M ctrl -M shift -k v -m shift -m ctrl -k Return
cat /tmp/pwm/got     # empty; "pasted" with the change below
```

**The change we run** (`nest.rs`, in place of `smithay::delegate_virtual_keyboard_manager!(State)`): a virtual keyboard
asked for on a seat that is not yours is made on yours, when the program has bound yours too; one that bound only an
agent's seat keeps it. Smithay's `Seat::client_seats` gives the program's own `wl_seat` of yours, and the request goes
on to smithay's handler unchanged otherwise. The whole patch is LatteOS's `patches/pleamar-wm-zzz-virtual-keyboard.patch`.

An alternative, if you would rather keep a program's choice: announce the agents' seats before yours (wtype, and most
tools that take one seat, keep the first or the last), or not announce them to programs that never get an agent's focus.
