TITLE
Bug: a group with a never-ending `shader:` leaves ghosts of what moved inside it (and tears glass cards)

BODY
**Version:** pleamar 0.7.27 (language 0.9), latte-goo scene on an AMD RX 640 (RADV POLARIS12), 1920 x 1080, Vulkan.

**What we did:** a `group { shader: breathe, ...; body { ... } }` whose WGSL (`shade(s)` returning `inside(s, p + amp * field(p, s.time))`)
bends the group's own picture a little with slow waves, so it never stops (it uses `s.time`). The group holds the goo (drops, the
level) and one outline body; the pointer's drop (an ellipse at `cursor.x, cursor.y`) is also drawn in it.

**What happens:** after the pointer has moved, a copy of its shape stays on the screen at every place it has been, never cleaned, and
glass cards beside tear. Same movement with the shader off: clean. The state is the same for every frame after (not a one-frame glitch).

**What we tried (none of it helped):**
- taking the pointer's shapes out of the group (ghosts remained, also of the pointer's own, separate body drawn after the group);
- `PLEAMAR_FULL_REPAINT=1` (ghosts remained), so it does not look like the damage of the swapchain frames but like the group's own
  picture that is not cleared where its content moved away.

**Expected:** what moved out of a shader group is cleaned from the group's picture, as without the shader.

**Reproduction:** not minimal yet: a group with a shader that reads `s.time`, an ellipse inside it that follows the pointer, and the
pointer moved across the screen. We can send the scene (goo/parts/body.plm, goo/shaders/breathe.wgsl in LatteOS-v0x-Jihlavanka).
